About

I am a tenured Senior Researcher at the German Youth Institute (Deutsches Jugendinstitut, DJI) in Munich. My work focuses on applied empirical research and the evaluation of public policies.

As an applied labor economist, my aim is to conduct relevant research that enables policymakers to make evidence-based decisions. In addition to topics in migration economics, I am particularly interested in the blessings and curses of the digital transformation, which includes the impact of technology diffusion on local economies and the impact of telework and digital media in families. I have a deep passion for data and econometric methods of causal inference and policy evaluation.

Research

My research uses empirical methods to study social and economic outcomes, family policies, and inequalities across individuals and families.
