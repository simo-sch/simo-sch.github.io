About

I am a Senior Researcher at the German Youth Institute (Deutsches Jugendinstitut, DJI) in Munich. My work focuses on applied empirical research and the evaluation of public policies.

My research interests include labor economics, policy evaluation, digitalization, education, migration, and family economics.

Research

My research uses empirical methods to study social and economic outcomes, family policies, and inequalities across individuals and families.
